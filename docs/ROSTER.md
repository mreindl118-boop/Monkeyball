# Roster casting plan

The casting table for all four bundled sets. Content authors write each card to the
Nova standard (docs/SPEC.md, "Reference character") and must keep the identity columns
exactly as listed here, so the roster composition rules hold across sets.

`attractedTo` uses the singular `Gender` values (`woman`, `man`, `nonbinary`).
`identity` is the always-visible identity line (never a secret, never a reveal).
`orientation` is shown only once the player has discovered the character's attractions.

## Composition check

- 30 characters: 12 women, 12 men, 6 nonbinary.
- Trans women: Vesper, Wren, Tamsin, Bea (4). Trans men: Theo, Felix, Eli (3).
- Nonbinary: Kai, Rook, Sol, Ash, Minh, Rowan (6).
- Orientation labels: lesbian 7, gay 7, bi 8, pan 8.
- Ace/demi spectrum: Priya (demi), Minh (ace), Maren (ace), Hollis (demi), Rowan (demi) — 5, three of them in Slow Burn.
- Styles: monogamous 9, open 5, polyamorous 8, flexible 8. Jealousy: compersion 5, low 8, medium 11, high 6.
